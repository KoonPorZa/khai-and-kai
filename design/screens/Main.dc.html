Khai means egg. Kai means chicken. Buy an egg, hatch it, and one registered bird becomes yours to care for. Every feeding and health check is posted on-chain.

How it works

  1. 1
    Get an egg
    0.01 tBNB. Up to 3 a day, and never more eggs than birds in the coop.
  2. 2
    Hatch it
    A block hash picks your bird. No bird is drawn twice, and you can check the draw yourself.
  3. 3
    Keep it cared for
    Pay the care fee each period. The farm posts what it did. A chicken with unpaid care can't change hands.
Testnet demo on BNB Smart Chain (chain 97). The 300 birds are sample data. Sponsorship is not legal ownership of an animal.
